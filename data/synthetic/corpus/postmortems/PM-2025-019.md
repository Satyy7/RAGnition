# PM-2025-019: Stock reservations delayed by up to 2 hours  
**Date:** 2025-09-09 | **Severity:** SEV2 | **Service:** inventory-api  

## Summary  
A slow query on the `inventory-db` caused the reservation consumer to process messages far slower than its poll interval configuration. This resulted in repeated consumer group rebalances, uncommitted batches being redelivered, and a growing reservation lag that peaked at two hours.

## Impact  
- Orders were accepted while stock reservations lagged up to 2 hours.  
- 312 orders were oversold because reservations were not applied in time.

## Detection  
The `ReservationLagHigh` alert fired at 14:25, indicating that the consumer lag had exceeded the defined threshold.

## Timeline  
- **14:10** – Bulk import into `inventory-db` completes.  
- **14:25** – `ReservationLagHigh` alert triggers.  
- **14:50** – Logs show `CommitFailedError` and repeated consumer group rebalances.  
- **16:05** – `ANALYZE` run on `inventory-db`; query time drops to ~20 ms and lag begins to drain.  
- **~16:30** – Lag fully drained after ~25 minutes.

## Root cause  
The consumer was configured with `max.poll.records=500`. After the bulk import, each message required ~1 s to process, making a single poll batch take ~500 s—exceeding `max.poll.interval.ms` (300 s). The consumer was removed from the group, partitions were rebalanced, and the uncommitted batch was redelivered. This cycle repeated, causing the lag to grow continuously.

## Trigger  
A slow query on `inventory-db` caused by missing statistics after a bulk import. The query latency increased to ~1 s per message.

## Resolution  
Running `ANALYZE` on `inventory-db` rebuilt the missing statistics, restoring query performance to ~20 ms. The consumer was then able to process messages within the poll interval, and the reservation lag drained in about 25 minutes.

## Contributing factors  
- Batch size (`max.poll.records=500`) was chosen without accounting for worst‑case per‑message processing time.

## Action items  
- Reduce `max.poll.records` to 50 (FUL-312, open – deprioritised).  
- Alert on consumer group rebalance rate (implemented).

## Lessons learned  
- Consumer poll configuration must consider the maximum possible processing time per message; large batch sizes can breach poll interval limits under degraded downstream performance.  
- Missing database statistics can dramatically increase query latency; post‑import maintenance (e.g., `ANALYZE`) should be part of the data‑load checklist.  
- Monitoring rebalance frequency provides early visibility into consumer health issues and can trigger faster investigation.
