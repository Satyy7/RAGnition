# CHG-2314: auth-service v4.2: 'remember me' sessions kept until sign-out

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Service:** auth-service
- **Type:** deploy
- **Team:** Identity
- **Status:** applied

## Details

Sessions created with remember_me=true are now stored in session-store without an expiry and deleted on sign-out. Other sessions keep the 1-day TTL. Requested by Storefront to reduce repeat sign-ins.
